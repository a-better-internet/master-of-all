MASTER·OF·ALL

A west-coast patchable modular built from every part of the Bastl Instruments library: the Trinity MONO FM, POLY and DRUM engines, the 6-track SQNCR, lfoExtended, the EarLevel exponential envelopes, the arpeggiator, tap tempo and the uncertainty buffer. It adds a complex oscillator, vactrol low pass gates, tape echo and reverb.

36 presets, including a bank of homages to the Latin American electronic and noise scenes of the 70s and 80s. Drag from any jack to another to patch. Save your own presets and record 24-bit WAV.

HOW TO PLAY

PATCHING

Drag from an output (dark jack) to an input, or from an input to an output. Drag a patched input away to pull its cable; click a cable to delete it. An input takes one cable; an output can feed many. A small dot on an input means it is normalled — something useful is connected internally until you patch over it (e.g. the drum voices listen to the SQNCR, the oscillators listen to the touch plate, the sequencers listen to the CLOCK). Feedback loops are allowed.

Jack colours: amber = audio, cyan = control voltage, pink = gates/triggers, green = pitch. Signal conventions: audio ±1, CV 0…1 or ±1, pitch 1.0 per octave, gates > 0.4 (amplitude carries velocity). Modules not reaching the OUTPUT are skipped by the engine (shown as idle).

SQNCR (drum sequencer)

6 instruments × 8 patterns × 64 steps × 4 substeps (64th notes). Choose an edit mode, then click steps: HIT, ACCent, SOFT, RATCHet (×2/×3/×4 64ths), SHIFT (late 32nd, 64th drag, flam), MUTE (keeps the notes), SKIP (inactive steps are jumped over). Each track has its own length (drag the number) for polymeters. T/G toggles TRIGGER or GATE events. Hold LOOP to stutter the current step. REC records the pads/keys 1–6 live with the chosen quantization. MOM makes pattern buttons momentary.

RECORDING

● REC captures the master output after the limiter, sample-accurately inside the audio thread, and saves a 24-bit stereo WAV with TPDF dither at the device sample rate. Takes stay in the TAKES menu until you reload the page.

SIGNAL CHAIN SAFETY

The whole rack runs per-sample in one AudioWorklet with no allocations in the audio loop. Parameters are smoothed, so knob moves don't make zipper noise. Oscillators are band-limited (PolyBLEP, 2× oversampled folding and FM), DC blockers sit on every output, and the OUTPUT has a true look-ahead peak limiter (ceiling −0.5 dBFS) so nothing clips. A NaN guard resets the engine if a patch ever goes unstable. Preset changes fade out and back in. If your machine stutters, set the buffer to safe.
